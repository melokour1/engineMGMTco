Your 3 tips: How to get into the top 3 on Google

01

Fix your Google Business Profile first

Google decides the top 3 mostly from your profile. Make sure your primary category is the exact service people search for (e.g. "Dentist", not "Health"), add every service you offer with a one-line description, fill in hours (including holiday hours), and add real photos of your location, team and work. Use the same business name, address and phone number you use everywhere else.

02

Get a steady flow of reviews that mention what you do

A few new reviews every month beats a burst once a year. Ask every happy customer the same day, with a direct review link (from your profile's "Ask for reviews" button). When they write it, they'll naturally mention the service and the area, and that helps you rank for both. Reply to every review, good or bad, within a couple of days.

03

Make your website back up your profile

Link your profile to a page that matches it: one page per main service, each saying what you do and the area you serve, with your name, address and phone number on it exactly as on Google. Make it easy to call or book from a phone in one tap.
